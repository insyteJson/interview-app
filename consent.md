### 2. Purpose

This study is part of a Bachelor's thesis. It tests whether a structured profile built from concrete examples of what a person has done tells recruiters more than a conventional CV, and whether candidates find it a more accurate picture of themselves.
The study is for research only. **No real application, hiring decision or job placement takes place.**

### 3. What taking part involves

1. You do **one structured interview in English, on your own, in this web page**. You type (or dictate) your answers. It takes roughly **45 to 90 minutes**, depending on how many roles you choose to discuss. 
2. You first enter the roles you want to talk about in a short form: jobs with company and dates, and, if you like, volunteering or hobbies. For each role you then say which tools or methods you used and describe specific situations you experienced. The questions are **fixed and shown by an automated system**; an AI does not choose or write them. Before some questions, the system may repeat a few of your own words back to you ("You said ..."); this short restatement is produced by the AI model.
3. **An AI model (Claude, by Anthropic) reads each answer** to decide which fixed question comes next. It does not write the questions.
4. Nothing is audio- or video-recorded. Your answers are saved as you typed them.
5. At the end you upload your CV as a PDF. **The page blacks out your name, contact details, links, photo and anything else you list** (for example an employer you prefer to hide), shows you every page, and only then saves the blacked-out version. Your original file is processed in the server's memory and **is not stored**. The CV is **not** sent to the AI model.
6. Afterwards, the AI model writes a one-page behavioural resume from your answers, and the study lead checks every entry against what you said. It shows your roles (with company, dates and tools), short descriptions of the situations you described, and labels describing the kind of behaviour each situation shows. It contains no ratings of you. You rate it and your CV on a short questionnaire, and both are shown to at most 10 recruiters for research purposes.

You may **skip any question**, **stop at any time**, or ask for any example to be **left out of your profile**, without giving a reason.

### 4. What data is processed

- Your answers exactly as you typed them, linked to your participant code (for example "P03"), not to your name. This includes the companies, dates and tools you name.
- Technical records of each step (time, which question was shown).
- Your questionnaire answers and your CV in its blacked-out form (the original upload is not stored; the study lead also checks it by hand).
- Your consent and a list linking your name to your participant code, kept **separately** from all study data.

**Please do not type names of other people.** Company names, job titles and places are fine and will appear in your resume, as on a CV. If you black out an employer in your CV, it is replaced by a general description in your resume as well. Nobody removes names for you while you type: **what you type is sent as you type it** to the services listed in section 6. The study lead removes names of people before the resume is created. Even without names, someone who knows you well could recognise a situation. This cannot be ruled out completely.

- **Anthropic PBC, USA (AI model "Claude", via its programming interface).** Every answer you type is sent to Claude. Because company names, job titles and achievements are shown, **a recruiter who knows you or your employers may be able to recognise you.** Recruiters agree not to try to identify candidates.

Risks of the transfer to the USA: US law may not offer a level of data protection equivalent to EU law; US authorities may, under certain conditions, be able to access data held by US companies; your GDPR rights may be harder to enforce against a company outside the EU.

### 8. Automated decisions and profiling

No automated decision within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR is made about you. The resume, written by the AI model and checked by the study lead, restates what you said, grouped into three themes, and labels the kind of behaviour each situation shows (for example "Organising & Executing"). It contains no scores, rankings or personality judgements and is not used in any real application.
