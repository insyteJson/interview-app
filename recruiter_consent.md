### 2. Purpose

This study is part of a Bachelor's thesis. It tests whether a structured resume built from concrete examples of what a person has done tells recruiters more than a conventional CV.
The study is for research only. **No real application, hiring decision or job placement takes place**, and your ratings have no consequences for any candidate.

### 3. What taking part involves

1. You take part **on your own, in this web page**. You need professional experience in recruiting or screening candidates; formal authority over hiring decisions is not required.
2. You are shown candidates one at a time. For each candidate you see **two documents, one after the other**: a conventional CV and a behavioural resume. You rate each document on short click questions, then answer one question comparing the two. There is **no vacancy**: please judge each document as evidence about the candidate as such.
3. **Please rate at least 3 candidates** (about 8 to 10 minutes each, so roughly 25 to 35 minutes). After that you may stop, or rate further candidates if you wish.
4. Your answers are saved after every step. You receive a **personal participant code and link** (for example "R04") so you can continue later; please keep it.
5. Nothing is audio- or video-recorded. **No AI model processes your answers.**
6. At the end you can create a personal invite link for a colleague, and you can optionally leave your email address to hear how the project develops. **The email is stored separately and is never linked to your answers.**

You may **skip any optional question**, **skip any candidate**, or **stop at any time**, without giving a reason.

### 4. Confidentiality of the candidate documents

The documents belong to **real people who agreed to take part**. They are shown under a participant code, without names or contact details, but company names, job titles and dates are visible.
- Please **do not save, download, screenshot, copy or share** the documents, and use them only for this study.
- Please **do not try to identify** any candidate.
- **If you think you recognise a candidate, click "I recognise this person, skip".** That candidate is then removed from your list and you get the next one.

### 5. What data is processed

- Your ratings and optional comments, linked to your participant code, **not to your name**.
- Your years of experience in recruiting and your hiring domain (for example "IT", "finance").
- Technical records of each step (time spent on each step, which candidate and document was shown, in which order).
- Your consent record (which statements you ticked, version of this text, time).

The study does not ask for your name, employer or contact details (the optional email in section 3, point 6, is stored separately and not linked to your answers). **Please do not type them, or names of other people, into the comment fields.**
